Cutesy Privacy Policy

Android Mobile Game

Effective date: October 2026

E Enterprise Studios ("we", "us", or "our") provides the mobile game Cutesy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, shared, and protected when you play Cutesy.

1. Information We Collect

Cutesy uses local game storage to maintain gameplay information such as level progress, earned coins, unlocked items, and player preferences.

Advertising providers integrated with Cutesy may process technical information, including device and application information, advertising identifiers where permitted, approximate location inferred from IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic data.

The information processed depends on your age, location, device settings, applicable laws, and privacy choices.

2. Advertising

Cutesy integrates Unity LevelPlay mediation with advertising services including Google AdMob, Unity Ads, and ironSource Ads.

The game may display optional rewarded video advertisements and interstitial advertisements. Players may choose to watch rewarded advertisements to receive in-game benefits, such as coins or unlockable items.

Cutesy is intended for a general audience, including players under the age of 13. We intend to provide child-appropriate advertising where permitted by applicable laws and platform policies.

Advertising eligibility, content, and data processing may vary depending on a player's age, region, and privacy settings.

3. Children's Privacy

Cutesy is intended for a general audience and may be played by children under 13 years old. We recognize that children's personal information requires additional protection.

Our intended advertising approach for children is to use child-appropriate advertisements where permitted, subject to applicable children's privacy laws and platform requirements. This includes restrictions on behavioral advertising and advertising-related data processing.

Advertising for children will only be enabled where the necessary protections and eligible advertising services have been verified.

Parents and legal guardians may contact us with questions or requests concerning their children's privacy.

4. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, Cutesy is designed to request appropriate consent for advertising-related data processing.

Depending on the player's region and the available privacy controls, players may be able to review or update advertising privacy choices.

Device advertising preferences may also be managed through Android settings. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily prevent all advertisements from appearing.

5. How Information Is Used

Information processed through Cutesy and its service providers may be used to:

  • Maintain game progress and preferences.
  • Provide gameplay features and rewards.
  • Deliver eligible advertisements.
  • Measure advertising performance where permitted.
  • Prevent fraud and maintain service security.
  • Diagnose technical problems where applicable.

6. Sharing and Third-Party Services

Cutesy integrates third-party advertising services. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies, applicable laws, and user privacy choices.

Advertising providers include:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Privacy Policy

Not every advertising provider or advertising feature will necessarily be available to every player or age group.

7. Data Retention and Security

Cutesy stores certain gameplay information locally on the player's device. This information may remain until the game's local data is cleared or the application is uninstalled, subject to device backup and restoration behavior.

Third-party advertising providers may retain information according to their own policies and applicable legal requirements.

We aim to limit information processing to what is reasonably necessary for gameplay, advertising, and operation of the game.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain uses of personal information.

You may contact E Enterprise Studios to submit a privacy-related request. Requests involving information controlled by third-party providers may need to be directed to those providers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in Cutesy, advertising services, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
